EFRA's annual youth programme

Ramsar Heroes

A 10-day summer intensive in ecology and urbanism for teenagers aged 14–17. Lectures from leading experts, field trips, volunteering at Karasu Lake — and a floating island for migrating birds, built by the participants themselves.

Why “Ramsar”

The programme is named after the Ramsar Convention, the international treaty on wetlands signed in the Iranian city of Ramsar in 1971. Wetlands — lakes, ponds, marshes and river floodplains — are among the most threatened ecosystems on the planet.

Our heroes learn that wetlands are not only far-away nature reserves. A small urban lake next to home is a wetland too, and it needs the same care. Each summer the programme has a central theme; in 2025 it was water resources, with a special focus on ponds and lakes in the city.

The 2025 programme was designed by Urban Forum Kazakhstan specially for Karasu Lake, the home of EFRA's first project.

  • Lectures

    Leading experts in urbanism, ecology, sustainability, landscape and the circular economy.

  • Tours and picnics

    Trips to study ecosystems: the Main Botanical Garden and the Pervomayskiye ponds.

  • Volunteering

    Clean-ups around Karasu Lake and along the way to the city's murals.

  • Masterclasses and research

    Water sampling, biodiversity observation, nature journaling and project skills.

  • A floating island for migrating birds

    Over three workshops the participants design and build a floating island and launch it on the lake.

A floating island, built by teenagers

A floating island is a buoyant mat with plants that grows on the water. It is easy to install, inexpensive and simple to maintain.

  1. For birds: a safe place to rest and nest away from people and dogs.
  2. For water: roots take up nutrients and help keep the lake clean.
  3. For fish: shade and shelter under the island.

Floating islands are one of the measures recommended in the Karasu Lake Conservation Plan. In 2025 the participants of Ramsar Heroes built one in three workshops with architect Asel Yeszhanova.
